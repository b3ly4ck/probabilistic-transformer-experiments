Covers the session from an empty repository to the first calibration runs on PTB. Written for

Everything below is reproducible from the repository; each run cites the commit it was produced

## 1. Headline

**The causal PT decoder is implemented and provably correct on every test available, and it does
not learn language modelling at calibration scale.** It converges to a perplexity
indistinguishable from a unigram model — and, decisively, it cannot fit its own training data.

That is not a scientific result about structure. Per `RESEARCH_PLAN.md` it is a return to
Experiment 0, and the next step is diagnosis, not a sweep.

Three findings that are results regardless of how the diagnosis goes:

1. Under exact inference, the Appendix B.3 global head `G_t` is **context-free by construction**
   and cannot act as a feed-forward layer. The FFN analogue exists only under mean field.
2. The exact readout costs **78× the mean-field readout** in wall-clock. §23.3's "same FLOPs,
   worse constants" understates it; the cost is memory traffic.
3. The mean-field PT decoder is only **1.3× GPT per step**. The construction is not expensive;
   the exact readout is.

## 3. Experiment 0 — validation

All nine checks pass. The two that matter most:

- **Check 7.** The worked example of `causal_pt_output_note.pdf` §5 reproduces **digit for
  digit**, including the intermediate attention logits `(.160, .413, .178, 1.776)` and
  `(.074, .387, .128, 1.890)`, and the final `p(W₄) = (.460, .267, .007, .267)`.
- **Check 9.** The exact readout agrees with brute-force enumeration over the joint to
  **7.5 × 10⁻⁸**. The slot is a tree and the factorisation is right.

Also established: the free energy is non-increasing and each update is an exact argmin, not
merely a descent step; causality holds bitwise; the input and output word matrices are one
object.

Two checks failed first for the right reasons and were rewritten rather than tuned:

- Position 0 has `D₀ = {ROOT}` and therefore no context at all, so a batch whose first tokens
  differ carries an irreducible `log 2` there. Measured 0.705 against `log 2 = 0.693`.
- The exact readout needed `d = 32` where mean field memorised at `d = 8`. Diagnosed as
  capacity, not optimisation: more steps, higher learning rate, more channels and more rounds
  did not close it; widening `d` did, immediately.

## 4. Two corrections to earlier assumptions

**The readout mainline was inverted.** §17.2 recommends MFVI; §23.3 in Part IV states the
verdict outright — *exact readout mainline, mean-field two-stream as the ablation* — and the
query stream disappears entirely. Read before implementation, so the code was written to the
later recommendation.

**"Frozen prefix" is not stop-gradient.** An earlier design proposed running the filtering pass
under `no_grad`. That is wrong: §18 Check 4 describes layer-parallel two-stream attention under
one triangular mask, and §25.1 frames freezing as *dropped evidence* — an inference-level
statement. `q̄ⱼ` is a conditioning constant of the variational problem at later slots, not a
tensor detached from autograd. Causality comes from the mask, exactly as in a transformer.
Running under `no_grad` would have starved `S` and `T` of the gradient that trains them.

**`T` is parameterised full-rank in this implementation.** `self.T` is `(h, d, d)`, hence `h·d²`.
§22.2 says *"decomposed `T` is linear in `d`"* and §18 Check 4 writes `T⁽ᶜ⁾ = U⁽ᶜ⁾V⁽ᶜ⁾ᵀ`, giving
`2·h·d·r`. At `d = 2048, h = 4` that is 16.8M against 1.05M at `r = 64` — a factor of 16. Every
budget number computed so far assumes the full-rank form. **Open decision:** implement the
factored form or state the deviation.

## 5. Experiment 1 — split into arms, and why

Wu & Tu report PT underperforming on large data and *suspect* the missing feed-forward
structure. They propose global variables (Appendix B.3) as an in-graph substitute and **never
test them** — B.3 is a derivation with no experiments. So `G_t` is a measured variable, not an
assumption: arm 1.1 runs without it, arm 1.2 with it, one code path, one flag.

The construction is **B.3.3 single-split** — a third subsection distinct from B.3.1 (all-dep)
and B.3.2 (dep-split): one global head per word, `B′ ∈ ℝ^{m×d}` shared across channels, their
Eq. (46). That is what §22.2 names.

### `G_t` is degenerate under exact inference

`G_t` is a leaf attached only to `Z_t`, so exact inference integrates it out:

```
log μ_t(a) = Σ_c LSE_{j ∈ D_t} B⁽ᶜ⁾[j,a]  +  LSE_k B′[k,a]
             └── depends on the prefix ──┘    └── constant in t ──┘
```

The second term is identical at every position and for every sentence: `m·d` parameters collapse
to `d` numbers that reweight the label prior and carry no context. Under mean field the same
variable gives `σ(Q_Z B′ᵀ) B′` — the GFU operator the paper compares to a transformer FFN.

**This is a result about the construction**, and it puts two of the paper's own recommendations
in tension: §23.3 makes the exact readout the mainline, and §22.2 proposes `G_t` as the answer to
the missing FFN. Both cannot hold at once. Arm 1.2 therefore runs under MFVI.

Measured consequence: with `G_t` attached the exact readout cannot memorise a single sequence —
0.4536 against 0.0021 without it — and it is out of reach of hyperparameters, with `λ_G` at 5, 20
and 100 giving a final loss identical to four decimals because `Q_G` converges near-uniform.

## 6. Calibration results

PTB, word level, `|V| = 10,000`, 929,589 training tokens. TITAN RTX. Every run's device was
audited; all reported runs used CUDA.

**Unigram baseline: val ppl 687.0.**

| Model | Params (total / emb / non-emb) | Steps | Best val PPL | Test PPL | Wall-clock | Peak mem |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GPT `d=160, L=4` | 2.85M / 1.62M / 1.23M | 2 000 | **130.63** | 118.56 | 37 s | 0.68 GiB |
| GPT, same | 2.85M / 1.62M / 1.23M | 20 000 | **131.35** | 2015.67 | 364 s | 0.68 GiB |
| PT MFVI, no `G_t` | 2.83M / 2.56M / 0.27M | 20 000 | **664.19** | 612.14 | 471 s | 0.70 GiB |
| PT MFVI, with `G_t` (`m=64`) | 2.85M / 2.56M / 0.29M | 20 000 | **678.40** | 621.29 | 483 s | 0.70 GiB |
| PT exact, no `G_t` | 2.83M / 2.56M / 0.27M | 300 | 7004.89 | 6976.89 | 549 s | 4.28 GiB |

Matched almost exactly on **total** parameters (2.83M vs 2.85M) and off by **4.5×** on
non-embedding (0.27M vs 1.23M) — which is precisely why the plan is now to sweep `d` and report
the curve rather than pick a matching convention.

### The two models fail in opposite directions

| | train ppl at 20 000 steps | best val ppl | behaviour |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| GPT | **5.4** | 131.35 | fits the corpus, then **overfits hard** — val ppl runs away to 2326 |
| PT | **611** | 664.19 | **cannot fit the training data at all**; val flat from step 10 000 |

This is the sharpest diagnostic obtained. PT's failure is not generalisation and not
regularisation: its *training* perplexity sits at the unigram level. It is underfitting by a
wide margin, at 88 epochs over the corpus, with the loss curve flat for the last half of
training.

### Samples

GPT, val ppl 130:

```
the company said third-quarter net fell N N to $ N million or N cents a share
the largest <unk> of west germany 's N N <unk> N and N N to the u.s.
```

PT, val ppl 664:

```
but 's will in that of an on has the will on on they which $ a a n't at
the which an it in with is it of be $ a at from to are be and $ its it is
```

Function words only, no content words, no local structure — exactly what a context-blind model
that has learned word frequencies produces. The two `G_t` variants emit **identical token
sequences** under the same seed.

### Cost

| Readout | ms/step | Peak memory | vs MFVI |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| MFVI | 23.6 | 0.70 GiB | — |
| exact, chunked at 250 | 1832 | 4.28 GiB | **78×** |

The exact readout materialises `(B, n, |V|, d)` and runs elementwise exponentials over it; the
matmul form materialises `(B, n, |V|)` and uses tensor cores. A 20 000-step exact run is ~10 h
against 8 min. The fused cross-entropy of §23.3 is not optional at sweep scale.

GPT is 18.5 ms/step, so **PT under MFVI is only 1.3× the baseline**.

## 7. What is unresolved

**Blocking — why PT cannot fit the training data.** Candidates, not yet separated, roughly 4–6
short runs at 8 minutes each:

- `λ_H = 1/d` at `d = 256` multiplies attention logits by 256. Checked at initialisation and the
  attention is *not* saturated (entropy 3.1 of 4.16 nats) — but not checked after training.
- `n_rounds = 3` may be too few for the inner loop to converge.
- The learning rate is shared with GPT by design; a model whose parameters are almost entirely
  one tied matrix may need a different one.
- The rank-`d` readout may bind harder than expected at `|V| = 10,000`, `d = 256`.

**Open decisions.** Whether to implement the factored `T`; the value of `λ_G` (not pinned by the
source — the paper's variance argument gives `1/d`, not `1/m`); the value of `m`.

**Deferred until PT learns.** The `d` sweep, the parameter-matching curve, Experiment 2 (Looped)
and Experiment 3 (exact vs MFVI). Sweeping a model that does not learn would measure nothing.

## 8. Operational notes

Worth recording because they cost time and would cost it again.

- A single Slurm job for four models would have hit the wall-clock limit on the exact-readout run
  alone and taken the other rows with it, since results are written at exit. One job per model.
- `ai_gpu32`'s GPU failed intermittently. After a job died on `nvidia-smi`, that check was made
  non-fatal — which removed the only guard against a broken GPU, and the next job **silently
  trained on CPU** for 16 minutes. The runner now aborts unless `--allow-cpu` is given.
- Slurm block-buffers redirected stdout, so unflushed progress lines make a running job look
  hung. The training loop now flushes.
- `torch 2.0.1+cu117` is already installed cluster-wide under `/public/software/anaconda3`; no
  CUDA wheel download is needed. It requires an explicit `torch.utils.checkpoint` import.
- The accessible Slurm account is `tukw-critical` on partition `critical`, not `ShangHAI`.
