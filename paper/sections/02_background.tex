\subsection{Variables and factors}

\FROMPDF{Wu \& Tu \S2.2 --- variable set and label semantics}
Each token position $t$ carries a label variable $\Zv{t}$ ranging over $\nlab$ discrete labels,
and $\nchan$ head variables $\Hv{t}{c}$, $c = 1,\dots,\nchan$, each ranging over candidate
head positions. The word at position $t$ is a variable $\Wv{t}$ over the vocabulary $\Vocab$,
observed in the encoder setting.

\FROMPDF{Wu \& Tu Eqs.\ 9/10, 14/21 --- ternary potential, distance clipping at $\clip$,
and the rank-$\krank$ decomposition $\Tfac{c} = \Ufac{c}\Vfac{c\top}$}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Sfac \in \R^{|\Vocab| \times \nlab}$ --- the word--label factor. Binary; in the
        encoder the word argument is clamped to the observed token. \emph{This factor is where
        \Cref{sec:output} does its work.}
  \item $\Tfac{c} = \Ufac{c}\Vfac{c\top}$ --- arc scores of channel $c$, Kruskal rank $\krank$.
  \item $\bfac$ --- the word unary term.
  \item distance-sensitive buckets, clipped at $\clip$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Mean-field inference and its computation graph}

\FROMPDF{Wu \& Tu \S2.3.3 and Appendix A.5 --- entropic Frank--Wolfe weights
$\lamZ,\lamH,\lamW,\lamG$}
Mean-field variational inference minimises the free energy $\Free$ over a factorised posterior
$\Q = \prod_t \QZ{t} \prod_{t,c} \QH{t}{c}$ by coordinate descent. Each coordinate update is a
softmax of a sum of expected potentials; unrolled for $\niter$ iterations, the resulting
computation graph has the shape of a $\niter$-layer transformer encoder with shared weights, in
which the attention distribution is $\QH{t}{c}$ --- a posterior over syntactic heads --- rather
than a learned function of queries and keys.
