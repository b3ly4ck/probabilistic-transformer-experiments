\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% Wu & Tu's Conclusion is one short paragraph restating model and headline result, with no new
% material. Match that -- roughly 0.2 of a column. Written last, to the measured result.

We have derived the autoregressive counterpart of the probabilistic transformer. Causality, a
single global energy and contextual dependence cannot hold together, so we replace the single
field by a directed chain of conditional random fields in which the prefix enters each step as a
frozen condition and the anti-causal term is never formed; and we obtain a language-model head
by unclamping a variable the model already had, which makes tied embeddings, the output bias
and a mixture-of-softmaxes readout consequences of the construction rather than additions to it.
