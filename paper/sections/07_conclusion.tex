\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We derived a causal probabilistic transformer: a directed chain of conditional random fields with
a frozen prefix, whose language-model head comes from unclamping the word variable. Its
perplexity is $\MBMinRatioGpt$ to $\MBMaxRatioGpt$ times a causal transformer's at the same
non-embedding budget, and most of the difference in scaling slope already appears with weight
sharing alone. Correcting the head temperature roughly doubles the usable label-set size, while
the exact readout and extra iterations did not help at this scale. Factored labels with the exact
readout and fixed-lag smoothing are natural next steps.
