\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The width ceiling moves but does not lift.}
Correcting the head temperature roughly doubles the usable label-set size, but at
$\nlab \in \{96, 128\}$ one seed in three collapses. A lower rank of the arc scores moves the
ceiling to $\nlab = \LadderTopLab$ at $\krank = \LadderTopRank$, yet at $\nlab = 256$,
$\krank = 64$ one seed in three still collapses. We have no mechanism for this ceiling, and
neither ladder should be extrapolated past the widths we measured.

\paragraph{The best repair is outside the graph.}
The standardised temperature is a normalisation outside the update equations, so the updates
are no longer centred gradients of a fixed per-slot free energy. The belief-norm temperature
stays inside the model but helps less (\Cref{fig:width}). Our best numbers therefore come from a
model that departs from the derivation in this one place; the label updates and the readout are
unaffected.

\paragraph{Scale, tuning and baselines.}
All hyperparameters are selected on the Penn Treebank, which has under a million training
tokens, and WikiText-2 serves only as a transfer check. On this corpus our baselines stop
improving above about $2\times10^5$ non-embedding parameters, which favours the smaller causal
PT. \citet{wu2023probabilistic} report the encoder falling behind beyond about $10^5$ sentences,
which is why we did not use WikiText-103, while \citet{kuang2026scaling} find it ahead at
matched budget once hyperparameters are transferred with maximal update parameterisation. We
have not tested which pattern the causal model follows at scale. Without dropout and with a
$64$-token context, all models are far from published PTB perplexities; the comparison holds
the protocol fixed and does not aim at the state of the art. The baselines also had a smaller
tuning grid (two learning rates) than the causal PT.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The undamped configuration at the original temperature is highly sensitive to last-bit
perturbations: its seed standard deviation is $\ReproUndampedSd$, and a run that reproduces to
the last printed digit on one GPU model ends $215$ perplexity away on another
(\Cref{sec:exp-repro}). Undamped configurations should be expected to differ on other hardware,
and differences of tens of perplexity there should not be read as results.

\paragraph{What the switch ladder cannot say.}
The all-PT model at the end of the switch ladder approximates the causal PT in four respects
(\Cref{app:grid-switches}), chiefly a free projection into label space where the causal PT reads
the label posterior directly. We cannot yet bound the gap between the all-PT model and the
causal PT: it is about $77$ perplexity at the best learning rate we ran, against about $170$ at
a single learning rate, and the all-PT model's learning-rate sweep has not turned over. The
ladder also runs four iterations and four channels, against three and two for the decoder, so
part of this gap is the depth cost of \Cref{sec:exp-depth}. The single-switch (S1) estimates do
not depend on this endpoint; the cumulative ladder should be read for its shape only.

\paragraph{The latent variables depend on the parameterisation.}
In the standardised configuration the head posterior prefers nearby heads, though half its mass
lies beyond distance three; in the damped configuration it collapses onto the previous word
(\Cref{app:interpretability}). The labels behave the other way: most are readable at
$\nlab=32$, but at $\nlab=96$, which gives the better perplexity, most are not. We found no
setting where capacity and interpretability are both best.

\paragraph{No sequence-level joint.}
Giving up the global energy (\Cref{sec:obstruction}) is what makes the model causal. Quantities
defined by a sentence-level joint, such as a partition function or an exact sequence likelihood
under bidirectional evidence, are therefore unavailable. The model is a valid directed joint and
filtering is the right object for a decoder, but this is a real restriction relative to the
encoder.
