\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The width ceiling moves but does not lift.}
Correcting the head temperature roughly doubles the label-set size at which the model trains
stably, and at $\nlab \in \{96, 128\}$ it becomes unstable again, with one collapsed seed in
three at each. We report the widths where it fails alongside those where it works, but we do not
have a mechanism for the second ceiling, and a reader should not extrapolate the corrected curve
past where we measured it.

\paragraph{The repair that works leaves the graph.}
Standardising the head logits is a normalisation applied outside the update equations. It
forfeits the property that every update is the centred gradient of a fixed per-slot free energy
--- the property that makes the rest of this paper's claims about inference meaningful --- and
the variant that stays inside the construction helps less (\Cref{sec:exp-width}). The model we
report the best numbers for is therefore not the model the derivation of \Cref{sec:model}
delivers, and we have marked which is which throughout rather than quietly reporting the better
one.

\paragraph{Scale, and one corpus for tuning.}
Everything is selected on the Penn Treebank, under a million training tokens, with WikiText-2 as
a transfer check only. Two consequences. Our baselines are flat in parameter count above roughly
$2\times10^5$ non-embedding parameters on this corpus, so the comparison is made in a regime
where the transformer has stopped improving --- a favourable regime for us, and we say so. And
\citet{wu2023probabilistic} report the encoder degrading beyond about $10^5$ sentences while
\citet{kuang2026scaling} report the opposite once the parameterisation transfers; which of those
the causal model follows at scale is untested here.

\paragraph{Reproducibility of the model class, not only of our runs.}
The configuration this project inherited is chaotic: a seed standard deviation of 130 perplexity,
and a validation trace that is identical to the last printed digit on one GPU model and 215
perplexity away on another (\Cref{sec:exp-repro}). We report three seeds and a device for
everything, but anyone comparing against our numbers on other hardware should expect the
undamped configurations to differ, and should not read a difference of tens of perplexity there
as a result.

\paragraph{What the switch ladder cannot say.}
The ladder's transformer endpoint is exact; its PT endpoint is an approximation with four named
gaps, chiefly that its mixture readout keeps a free projection into label space where the real
model reads the label posterior directly. We measure the residue --- the difference between that
endpoint and the real decoder at the same width, corpus, loop and budget --- and it bounds how
much weight the per-switch attributions can carry.

\paragraph{The head variables have not learned dependency structure.}
The construction's interpretive claim has two halves and only one of them survives measurement.
Several label variables are recognisably syntactic with nothing supervising them
(\Cref{app:interpretability}). The head posterior is not: it places $0.976$ of its positional
mass at distance $1$ and $0.486$ of its total mass on \textsc{root}, so what the model has
learned is locality with a large sink, not attachment. Everything this paper says about the arc
factors is therefore a statement about a mechanism the trained model barely exercises, and a
reader should not take the dependency reading of $\Hv{}{}$ on trust from the derivation.

\paragraph{No single sequence-level joint.}
Giving up the global energy (\Cref{sec:obstruction}) is what makes the model causal, and it means
quantities defined by a sentence-level joint --- a partition function, an exact sequence
likelihood under bidirectional evidence --- are simply unavailable. The model is a valid directed
joint and filtering is the semantically correct object for a decoder, but this is a real
restriction relative to the encoder it descends from, not a technicality.
