\section{Derivations}
\label{app:derivations}

\subsection{Full mean-field updates}
\label{app:full-updates}

Fix a slot $t$. The prefix is frozen and contracted into the scores $\Bfac{c}_{j,a}$ of
\Cref{eq:contract}, with $\Bfac{c}_{\textsc{root},a} = \rfac{c}_a$. The live variables are
$\QW{t}$ on $\Vocab$, $\QZ{t}$ on the $\nlab$ labels, and one $\QH{t}{c}$ on $\Dep{t}$ per
channel. The expected negative log of \Cref{eq:unclamp} under their product gives, up to a
constant, the per-slot energy
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:slot-energy}
\begin{aligned}
E_t &= -\textstyle\sum_w \QW{t}(w)\,\bfac_w\\
    &\quad - \textstyle\sum_{w,a} \QW{t}(w)\,\QZ{t}(a)\,\Sfac_{w,a}\\
    &\quad - \textstyle\sum_c \sum_{j\in\Dep{t}} \sum_a
             \QH{t}{c}(j)\,\QZ{t}(a)\,\Bfac{c}_{j,a}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
$E_t$ is multilinear, so each coordinate step has a closed form. It is also one energy per slot.
The prefix enters only through the constants $\Bfac{c}_{j,a}$, and the factors in which slot $t$
serves a later slot $u$ belong to $E_u$, where $\qbar{t}$ is a constant. No term is deleted, and
the anti-causal term is never formed (\Cref{rmk:never-formed}); this is how the construction
respects \Cref{thm:trilemma}. The gradients are
%
\begin{align}
\label{eq:grad-W}
  \frac{\partial E_t}{\partial \QW{t}(w)}
    &= -\bfac_w - \textstyle\sum_a \QZ{t}(a)\,\Sfac_{w,a},\\
\label{eq:grad-H}
  \frac{\partial E_t}{\partial \QH{t}{c}(j)}
    &= -\textstyle\sum_a \QZ{t}(a)\,\Bfac{c}_{j,a},\\
\label{eq:grad-Z}
  \frac{\partial E_t}{\partial \QZ{t}(a)}
    &= -\textstyle\sum_w \QW{t}(w)\,\Sfac_{w,a}\\
    &\quad - \textstyle\sum_c \sum_{j\in\Dep{t}} \QH{t}{c}(j)\,\Bfac{c}_{j,a}.\nonumber
\end{align}
%
Inference minimises the free energy
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:slot-free-energy}
\begin{aligned}
  \Free_t = E_t &- \lamW \Ent(\QW{t}) - \lamZ \Ent(\QZ{t})\\
                &- \lamH \textstyle\sum_c \Ent\big(\QH{t}{c}\big),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
the extended entropic Frank--Wolfe objective of \citet{wu2023probabilistic} (their
Appendix~A.2), which is the ordinary mean-field free energy when all three weights are one.
Each coordinate subproblem $\min_{z\in\Delta}\langle c,z\rangle + \lambda\sum_s z_s\log z_s$ is
solved by $z = \softmax(-c/\lambda)$ (their Appendix~A.3), which gives the updates
%
\begin{align}
\label{eq:full-update-H}
  \QH{t}{c}(j) &\propto \exp\Big(\tfrac{1}{\lamH}
      \textstyle\sum_a \QZ{t}(a)\,\Bfac{c}_{j,a}\Big),\\
\label{eq:full-update-Z}
  \QZ{t}(a) &\propto \exp\Big(\tfrac{1}{\lamZ}\Big[
      \textstyle\sum_w \QW{t}(w)\,\Sfac_{w,a}\\
    &\qquad\quad + \textstyle\sum_c\sum_{j\in\Dep{t}}
      \QH{t}{c}(j)\,\Bfac{c}_{j,a}\Big]\Big),\nonumber\\
\label{eq:full-update-W}
  \QW{t}(w) &\propto \exp\Big(\tfrac{1}{\lamW}\Big[
      \bfac_w + \textstyle\sum_a \QZ{t}(a)\,\Sfac_{w,a}\Big]\Big).
\end{align}
%
A softmax ignores a constant shift, so only the centred gradient is read and the dropped
constant has no effect. \Cref{eq:full-update-H,eq:full-update-Z} are
\Cref{eq:update-H,eq:update-Z} with the word message $\sum_w \QW{t}(w)\Sfac_{w,a}$ in place of
the observed unary. The softmax of \Cref{eq:full-update-W} over $\Vocab$ is the output layer.

\paragraph{Schedule.}
Each variable is initialised from its own unary-type factors (Equation~7 of Wu and Tu):
$\QW{t}^{(0)} \propto \exp(\bfac)$ and $\QZ{t}^{(0)} \propto \exp(\tilde{s}/\lamZ)$ with
$\tilde{s}_a = \sum_w \QW{t}^{(0)}(w)\Sfac_{w,a}$, the prior-weighted mean embedding that plays
the role of a \textsc{[mask]} token (\Cref{sec:inference}). We then update $\{\QH{t}{c}\}$ and $\QZ{t}$ for $\rho$ rounds in their
asynchronous order (their \S2.3.2) and read out with \Cref{eq:full-update-W}. At $\rho=1$ the
attention query is the fixed learned probe $\softmax(\tilde{s}/\lamZ)$; with $\rho \ge 2$ it
depends on the context. Further $W\!\leftrightarrow\!Z$ rounds are valid mean field but feed the
model's own word guess back into the label belief, so we treat them as an ablation.

\paragraph{The observed mode is the same energy.}
Clamping $\QW{t}$ to the point mass $\delta_{w_t}$ makes \Cref{eq:full-update-W} idle and
reduces \Cref{eq:slot-energy}, up to the constant $-\bfac_{w_t}$, to
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:obs-energy}
\begin{aligned}
  E_t^{\text{obs}} &= -\textstyle\sum_a \QZ{t}(a)\,\Sfac_{w_t,a}\\
    &\quad -\textstyle\sum_c \sum_{j\in\Dep{t}} \sum_a
      \QH{t}{c}(j)\,\QZ{t}(a)\,\Bfac{c}_{j,a},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
the per-position energy of the chain in \Cref{sec:chain}, whose updates are
\Cref{eq:update-H,eq:update-Z}. Encoding and decoding are thus one energy family under two
conditioning patterns (\Cref{fig:factor-graph}), with $\Sfac$ trained in both roles. By
\Cref{eq:grad-H} the head gradient contains no word message, so the attention update is the same
function in both modes. Sharing one attention pattern between the predictive and the observed
mode at a slot is therefore a choice of initialisation, which mean field leaves free.

\subsection{Derivation of the exact readout}
\label{app:exact-derivation}

With the prefix frozen, the live factors at slot $t$ are the word unary $\bfac$, the word--label
factor $\Sfac$ and one contracted arc factor per channel. They form a star centred on $\Zv{t}$,
with leaves $\Wv{t}$ and $\Hv{t}{1},\dots,\Hv{t}{\nchan}$. A star is a tree, so one sweep of
sum--product gives the exact marginal. Each head variable appears in one term and has no unary,
so eliminating it is a plain sum over $\Dep{t}$, and the channels separate:
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:elim-H}
\begin{aligned}
  \sum_{j_1,\dots,j_\nchan \in \Dep{t}}
    \exp\Big(\textstyle\sum_c \Bfac{c}_{j_c,a}\Big)
  &= \prod_c \sum_{j\in\Dep{t}} e^{\Bfac{c}_{j,a}}\\
  &=: \readout{t}(a).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
In logarithms, channel $c$ sends $\LSE_{j\in\Dep{t}} \Bfac{c}_{j,a}$ to $\Zv{t}$, seeded at
$j = \textsc{root}$ by $\rfac{c}_a$. Eliminating $\Zv{t}$ leaves
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:elim-Z}
\begin{aligned}
  \hat{p}\big(\Wv{t}{=}w \mid w_{\prefix{t}}\big)
    &\;\propto\; \textstyle\sum_a e^{\bfac_w + \Sfac_{w,a}}\,\readout{t}(a)\\
    &\;=\; e^{\bfac_w} \textstyle\sum_a e^{\Sfac_{w,a}}\,\readout{t}(a),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
which is \Cref{eq:exact-readout}. The implementation works with the logits
$\bfac_w + \LSE_a\,(\Sfac_{w,a} + \log\readout{t}(a))$ and never forms $\readout{t}$ itself.
With $\Znorm_a = \sum_{w} e^{\bfac_{w} + \Sfac_{w,a}}$ and
$p(w \mid a) = e^{\bfac_w + \Sfac_{w,a}}/\Znorm_a$, \Cref{eq:elim-Z} reads
$\hat p(\cdot) = \sum_a \pi_t(a)\, p(\cdot \mid a)$ with mixture weights
$\pi_t(a) \propto \readout{t}(a)\,\Znorm_a$. The $\nlab$ components are fixed and only the
weights depend on the context. This makes the readout a mixture of softmaxes
(\Cref{sec:cmp-readout}), weaker than one whose components also depend on the context.

\paragraph{The sequence form.}
Since $\Dep{t+1} = \Dep{t}\cup\{t\}$, the channel message of \Cref{eq:elim-H} is a causal prefix
log-sum-exp in $t$. One \texttt{logcumsumexp} scan along the positions computes it for all $t$
in $O(\seqlen\nlab\nchan)$, against $O(\seqlen^2\nlab\nchan)$ slot by slot. Clipped distances
keep this form. The causal model keeps the half $t-j>0$ of the clip function of
\citet{wu2023probabilistic}: distances $1,\dots,\clip$ each have a bucket and longer distances
share the last, so there are $\clip+1$ buckets. Writing
$\Bfac{c,k}_{j,a} = \sum_b \qbar{j}(b)\,\Tfac{c,k}_{a,b}$ for the contraction against the arc
matrix of bucket $k$,
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mu-scan}
\begin{aligned}
  \log\readout{t}^{(c)}(a) = \LSE\Big(\;&\rfac{c}_a,\;\;
      \LSE_{1\le j\le t-\clip-1} \Bfac{c,\clip+1}_{j,a},\\[-2pt]
    &\Bfac{c,1}_{t-1,a},\;\dots,\;\Bfac{c,\clip}_{t-\clip,a}\Big),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
where $\log\readout{t}(a) = \sum_c \log\readout{t}^{(c)}(a)$ and a term whose position index
falls below $1$ is $-\infty$. Only the far bucket accumulates, as one \texttt{logcumsumexp}
offset by $\clip+1$. Each near bucket holds one position per slot and is its own table shifted
by its distance, so the readout is one log-sum-exp over $\clip+2$ aligned tensors. The global
variable of \Cref{app:globals}, one more leaf off $\Zv{t}$, adds the term
$\LSE_k \Bglob_{k,a}$ to $\log \readout{t}(a)$.

\paragraph{The layer-parallel schedule.}
The exact readout keeps this schedule. The readout is computed in closed form from the final
prefix beliefs, the scan is an associative prefix reduction over $j<t$, and there is no
predictive inner loop, so a layer stays one triangular-masked pass (\Cref{sec:variants}). At
generation time the readout caches a running $\nlab \times \nchan$ table of channel messages,
extended by one \texttt{logaddexp} per generated token.

\subsection{Message weights}
\label{app:message-weights}

The weights $\lamZ,\lamH,\lamW$ of \Cref{eq:slot-free-energy}, and $\lamG$ for the global
variable of \Cref{app:globals}, each weight the entropy of one variable. Changing one changes
the objective, so a sweep over $\lamH$ is a sweep over objectives, not over a temperature within
one objective.

\paragraph{$\lamZ$ and $\lamH$.}
\citet{wu2023probabilistic} fix $\lamZ = 1$ and derive $\lamH = 1/\nlab$ from a variance
argument (their Appendix~A.5) in the manner of \citet{vaswani2017attention}. If the arc scores
are independent with mean zero and variance $\sigma^2$, the head logit
$\sum_{a,b} \QZ{t}(a)\,\qbar{j}(b)\,\Tfac{c}_{a,b}$ of \Cref{eq:full-update-H} has variance
$\sigma^2/\nlab^2$ at uniform beliefs, and dividing by $\lamH = 1/\nlab$ restores $\sigma^2$.
The causal head logit has the same form, with one belief frozen, so $\lamH = 1/\nlab$ carries
over. For $\lamZ$ their argument gives no value: the variance of the label message depends on the
sentence length, so they set $\lamZ=1$. In the causal model the label message sums over
$\Dep{t}$, whose size is $t$ (our extension of their argument), so no fixed $\lamZ$ can restore
the variance; we set $\lamZ=1$. The encoder's factor of two from symmetrising over head--child
order is absent, since the directed model traverses each arc once.

\paragraph{$\lamW$.}
The word weight has no counterpart in Wu and Tu's encoder, and none in the exact readout, which
eliminates $\Wv{t}$ exactly. Under the mean-field readout, tying lets the scale of $\Sfac$ set
both the output logits and the unary, so a value $\lamW \ne 1$ cannot be absorbed into $\Sfac$
and would temper the readout. We use $\lamW=1$ throughout; at any other value the
evaluation-time swap of \Cref{sec:exp3} would measure a temperature as well as the
approximation.

\paragraph{$\lamG$.}
No argument fixes the weight of the global variable, and Wu and Tu write its update (their
Appendix~B.3) without one. We set $\lamG = 1$ without derivation and did not sweep it.

\paragraph{Calibration.}
The variance argument holds at uniform beliefs, where the model starts but does not stay.
\Cref{sec:temperature} analyses the consequence.

\subsection{Complexity}
\label{app:complexity}

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
Layer & Width term & Length term \\
\midrule
Transformer layer          & $12\dmodel^2$ & $2\seqlen\dmodel$ \\
PT layer, observed mode    & $3\nlab^2$    & $2\seqlen\nlab$   \\
PT, mean-field, both modes & $5\nlab^2$    & $4\seqlen\nlab$   \\
PT, exact readout          & $3\nlab^2$    & $2\seqlen\nlab$   \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Approximate FLOPs per token and per layer, with $\krank\nchan\approx\nlab$ and
$\nlab=\dmodel$. A transformer's $12\dmodel^2$ is $4\dmodel^2$ for the query, key, value and
output projections plus $8\dmodel^2$ for the feed-forward block. The causal PT's $3\nlab^2$ is
$3\nlab\krank\nchan$ (prefix keys, which are also values, the query contraction and the output
contraction). The exact-readout row adds, per token, $O(\nlab\nchan)$ for the scan and
$O(|\Vocab|\nlab)$ for the vocabulary readout.}
\label{tab:complexity}
\end{table}

\Cref{tab:complexity} counts one layer at one position. A causal PT layer costs roughly half a
transformer layer, because it has no feed-forward block and no projections. Running the
mean-field readout, which solves a second inference problem in predictive mode at every slot,
costs about $0.4$--$0.6\times$ a transformer layer. Global variables add about $2\nglob\nlab$
per token. The vocabulary readout is an $O(|\Vocab|\nlab)$ log-sum-exp instead of a matrix
product, with the same FLOPs and worse hardware constants. The implementation chunks it over the vocabulary,
as a fused cross-entropy does, so the $|\Vocab|\times\nlab$ intermediate is never held in full.

Because the causal PT shares its parameters across iterations, depth and parameters cannot both
be matched against a transformer. FLOPs also do not predict wall-clock time. \citet{wu2023probabilistic}
measure their encoder at about three times slower than a transformer despite far fewer
parameters, and attribute this to gathering the arc matrices from the clipped distance table for
every position pair (their Appendix~C).
