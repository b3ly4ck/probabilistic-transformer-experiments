\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Stated here rather than in a footnote, because each is a consequence of the construction and
none is expected to disappear with more tuning.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The rank-$\nlab$ bottleneck.} All predictive information reaches the vocabulary
        through $\Zv{t}$ (\Cref{sec:bottleneck}).
  \item \textbf{Small-corpus regime.} \Cref{sec:setup} explains why the corpus is small; the
        behaviour of the causal PT beyond that regime is untested here, and the encoder is
        reported to degrade there.
  \item \textbf{No single sequence-level joint.} The chain of conditional CRFs gives up the
        global energy (\Cref{sec:construction}), so quantities defined by that joint are not
        available.
  \item \textbf{Hardware constants.} The vocabulary readout is a log-sum-exp rather than a
        matmul: the same FLOPs on worse constants (\Cref{sec:cost}).
