\subsection{The design that was rejected}
\label{sec:rejected-projection}

The obvious way to obtain next-token logits is to project the label posterior $\QZ{t}$ through
a matrix $\R^{\nlab \times |\Vocab|}$. We report this design because rejecting it is what
forced the correct one: such a matrix corresponds to no factor in the graph. The output would
then not traverse the factor graph at all, and the model would be a CRF with a neural head
bolted on --- precisely the thing the Probabilistic Transformer exists to avoid.
