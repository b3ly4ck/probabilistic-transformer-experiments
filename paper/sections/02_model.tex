\section{Causal Probabilistic Transformers}
\label{sec:model}

We recall the model of \citet{wu2023probabilistic}, show why masking it is not enough, and build
the causal model.

\subsection{Background}
\label{sec:recap}

A probabilistic transformer is a CRF over two families of discrete latent variables. Each
position $i$ of a sentence has a \emph{label} variable $\Zv{i}$ over $\nlab$ values (the
model's \emph{width}), read as a syntactic category, and $\nchan$ \emph{head} variables $\Hv{i}{1..\nchan}$, one per
\emph{channel}, each over the candidate heads of $i$. A unary factor
$\exp(\Sfac_{w_i, \Zv{i}})$ ties the label at $i$ to the observed word through one matrix
$\Sfac \in \R^{|\Vocab| \times \nlab}$, and a ternary factor
$\exp\big(\ind{\Hv{i}{c}{=}j}\, \Tfac{c}_{\Zv{i},\Zv{j}}\big)$ scores the labels at $i$ and at
its head $j$ in channel $c$.

Unrolled for $\niter$ iterations, mean-field inference is scaled dot-product attention (the head
update) followed by a re-injected unary (the label update): a $\niter$-layer transformer encoder
with shared weights and no feed-forward block. The word $w_i$ enters the model in one place
only: the unary factor $\Sfac_{w_i,\cdot}$.

\subsection{Why a causal mask is not enough}
\label{sec:obstruction}

Masking the ternary factors so that $\Hv{i}{c}$ can only point to positions $j < i$ does not
give a causal model, because three properties cannot hold together. \emph{Single global energy}
(SE): the updates are $\Q_x \propto \exp(-\nabla_x E / \lambda_x)$ for one twice continuously
differentiable energy $E$, with the
words entering only through terms local to a single position. \emph{Causality} (C2): for every
schedule and every node, the update at position $i$ does not read the state of any node at a
position $j > i$. \emph{Contextuality} (X): some belief at some position changes when a word at
another position changes.

\begin{theorem}[Trilemma]
\label{thm:trilemma}
No scheme satisfies SE, C2 and X simultaneously. Any two of the three are realisable.
\end{theorem}

If $E$ is twice continuously differentiable, $\partial^2 E / \partial \Q_i \partial \Q_j$ is
symmetric, so the term by which the label at $j$ reaches the update at $i$ and the reverse term
are one Hessian entry. Causality deletes one of them and symmetry then deletes the other. The
proof is in \Cref{app:proof}. The theorem needs the architectural form of causality, C2, which
masked attention satisfies; under a weaker trajectory form, C1, it fails
(\Cref{app:prop-sharp}). It also covers only context that passes through inference: an energy
with terms that read words at other positions could condition on the prefix directly. We
therefore give up the single global energy.

\begin{remark}[The anti-causal term]
\label{rmk:never-formed}
Deleting the anti-causal contribution from the symmetric model gives a field that is not the
gradient of any energy, and by \Cref{thm:trilemma} no repair of it can be. In the chain below,
the factor in which position $i$ heads a later word $j$ lives inside
$p(\Zv{j}, \Hvar{j} \mid z_{\prefix{j}})$, where $z_i$ is a conditioning value, so that
contribution never exists.
\end{remark}

\subsection{A directed chain of conditional CRFs}
\label{sec:chain}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\resizebox{0.76\columnwidth}{!}{\input{figures/fig1_factor_graph}}
\caption{The same factor graph in its two conditioning patterns ($\seqlen=3$); shaded nodes are
observed. (a) Encoder: every word is clamped and arcs are undirected, as in
\citet{wu2023probabilistic}. (b) Decoder at slot $t=3$: prefix labels are frozen conditions
(boxed), arcs point backwards, and the unclamped word $\Wv{3}$ carries the next-token
distribution. Factors and parameters are the same in both panels.}
\label{fig:factor-graph}
\end{figure}

Instead of one CRF over the sentence we define a chain of conditional CRFs, one per position.
The CRF at position $i$ ranges over $\big(\Zv{i}, \Hv{i}{1..\nchan}\big)$ and is conditioned on
the realised prefix labels $z_{\prefix{i}}$:
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:chain}
\begin{aligned}
  &p\big(\Zv{i}, \{\Hv{i}{c}\}_c \mid z_{\prefix{i}}, w_{1:i}\big) \\
  &\quad \propto
     \exp\Big(\Sfac_{w_i,\Zv{i}} \\[-4pt]
  &\qquad
      + \textstyle\sum_{c}\sum_{j \in \Dep{i}} \ind{\Hv{i}{c}{=}j}\, \tau^{(c)}_{\Zv{i}}(j)\Big),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
with head domain $\Dep{i} = \{\textsc{root}, 1, \dots, i-1\}$, arc score
$\tau^{(c)}_a(j) = \Tfac{c}_{a, z_j}$ for $j<i$, and $\tau^{(c)}_a(\textsc{root}) = \rfac{c}_a$ a
learned root column. The joint is the chain-rule product
$p(z, h \mid w) = \prod_i p(z_i, h_i \mid z_{\prefix{i}}, w_{1:i})$, a valid probability model by
construction, and, as \Cref{thm:trilemma} requires, there is no single global energy. The root
column gives the first word something to attend to, much as a beginning-of-sequence token does
in a transformer.

\paragraph{Inference is variational filtering.}
We process $i = 1,\dots,\seqlen$ and keep frozen filtering marginals
$\qbar{j} \approx p(\Zv{j} \mid w_{1:j})$ for $j<i$. Because the log-potential is linear in the
one-hot encoding of $z_j$, its expectation under $\qbar{j}$ is obtained by substituting
$\qbar{j}$ for that encoding:
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:contract}
  \E_{\qbar{j}}\big[\Tfac{c}_{a,\Zv{j}}\big]
    = \textstyle\sum_b \qbar{j}(b)\, \Tfac{c}_{a,b} =: \Bfac{c}_{j,a}.
\end{equation}
%
Step $i$ is therefore mean-field inference on a small CRF with unary $\Sfac_{w_i,\cdot}$
and binary factors $\exp(\Bfac{c}_{j,a})$, with updates
%
\begin{align}
\label{eq:update-H}
  \QH{i}{c}(j) &\propto \exp\Big(\tfrac{1}{\lamH}\textstyle\sum_a \QZ{i}(a)\, \Bfac{c}_{j,a}\Big),\\
\label{eq:update-Z}
  \QZ{i}(a) &\propto \exp\Big(\tfrac{1}{\lamZ}\big[\Sfac_{w_i,a} + \mathcal{G}_i(a)\big]\Big),
  \nonumber\\
  \mathcal{G}_i(a) &= \textstyle\sum_c\sum_{j\in\Dep{i}} \QH{i}{c}(j)\, \Bfac{c}_{j,a}.
\end{align}
%
With $\Tfac{c}=\Ufac{c}\Vfac{c}{}^{\top}$ this is masked scaled dot-product attention: the query
$\QZ{i}\Ufac{c}$ comes from the current position and the keys $\qbar{j}\Vfac{c}$ from frozen
prefix states, so the key--value cache follows from freezing the prefix. Frozen means constant
for step $i$'s inference problem, not detached in automatic differentiation: training gradients
flow through $\Bfac{c}_{j,\cdot}$ into the prefix computation, as through cached activations in
a causal transformer, and this is how $\Sfac$ is trained in both of its roles. This affects only
parameter learning: in the forward pass no belief at a position depends on a later word
(\Cref{app:validation}).

\subsection{The output mechanism}
\label{sec:output}

\paragraph{Unclamping the word variable.}
We call the position whose word is being predicted the \emph{slot}. A new
$\nlab\times|\Vocab|$ projection of $\QZ{t}$ would give next-token logits, but it would
correspond to no factor, $\QZ{t}$ describes the word already observed at $t$, and no factor
links $\Zv{t}$ to the next word, since arcs connect a label only to its head's label. The only
factor touching a word variable is the word--label factor, and the only factors
touching a label are that one and its arcs, so any route from prefix to vocabulary that stays in
the graph must be
$\qbar{\prefix{t}} \xrightarrow{\ \text{arcs}\ } \Zv{t} \xrightarrow{\ \Sfac\ } \Wv{t}$. Making
each word a variable turns the chain into a joint over words,
$p(w,z,h) = \prod_t p(\Wv{t}, \Zv{t}, \Hvar{t} \mid z_{\prefix{t}})$, with step conditional
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:unclamp}
\begin{aligned}
 &p\big(\Wv{t}, \Zv{t}, \Hvar{t} \mid z_{\prefix{t}}\big) \\
 &\quad \propto
   \exp\Big(\underbrace{\bfac_{\Wv{t}}}_{\text{word unary}}
   + \underbrace{\Sfac_{\Wv{t},\Zv{t}}}_{\text{word--label factor}} \\[-4pt]
 &\qquad
   + \textstyle\sum_c \sum_{j\in\Dep{t}} \ind{\Hv{t}{c}{=}j}\, \Tfac{c}_{\Zv{t}, z_j}\Big).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
No factor or parameter is added: the unary $\exp(\Sfac_{w_t,\Zv{t}})$ of \Cref{sec:recap} is
the binary factor $\exp(\Sfac_{\Wv{t},\Zv{t}})$ with $\Wv{t}$ clamped to the observed word.

\paragraph{Consequences.}
\textit{(i) Tying is forced.} The step conditional has one word--label factor, used both to
condition on $\Wv{t}{=}w_t$ and to predict it. Separate matrices for the two roles would be two
factors on the same pair of variables, and
$\exp(\Sfac_{w,a})\exp(\Sfac'_{w,a}) = \exp((\Sfac{+}\Sfac')_{w,a})$ collapses them into one.
Untying gives a different model.
\textit{(ii) The output bias is the word unary} $\bfac$, a per-word constant; in our
experiments it is fixed to the log unigram frequency of the training split.
\textit{(iii) One model, two conditioning patterns.} We call the pattern in which $\Wv{t}$ is
clamped the \emph{observed mode} and the one in which it is latent the \emph{predictive mode}.
The observed-mode energy is the predictive-mode energy with $\QW{t}$ clamped to $\delta_{w_t}$
(\Cref{fig:factor-graph}).

\subsection{Inference at a slot, and the exact readout}
\label{sec:inference}

At slot $t$, with the prefix frozen and contracted by \Cref{eq:contract}, the updates of
\Cref{eq:update-H,eq:update-Z} gain a word update
$\QW{t}(w) \propto \exp\big(\tfrac{1}{\lamW}[\bfac_w + \sum_a \QZ{t}(a)\Sfac_{w,a}]\big)$, whose
softmax is the language-model output layer. The word variable starts from its unary, so the
initial query is a prior-weighted mean embedding that plays the role of a \textsc{[mask]} token
(\Cref{app:full-updates}).

The slot's factor graph is a star centred on $\Zv{t}$, with edges $\Wv{t}\!-\!\Zv{t}$ and
$\Hv{t}{c}\!-\!\Zv{t}$, hence a tree, so sum--product is exact and has a closed form.

\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:exact-readout}
For the slot conditional of \Cref{eq:unclamp} with the prefix frozen,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:exact-readout}
\begin{aligned}
  \hat{p}(\Wv{t}{=}w \mid w_{\prefix{t}}) &\propto e^{\bfac_w}\textstyle\sum_a e^{\Sfac_{w,a}}\readout{t}(a),\\
  \log \readout{t}(a) &= \textstyle\sum_{c=1}^{\nchan} \LSE_{j \in \Dep{t}} \Bfac{c}_{j,a},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with the log-sum-exp seeded at $j=\textup{\textsc{root}}$ by $\rfac{c}_a$.
\end{proposition}

This is a mixture of $\nlab$ fixed softmax components with context-dependent weights, so it
escapes the rank constraint of the mean-field readout $\exp\E_{\QZ{t}}[\Sfac_{w,\cdot}]$
(\Cref{sec:cmp-readout}). Since $\Dep{t}$ grows by one position per step, one
\texttt{logcumsumexp} scan computes it in $O(\seqlen\nlab\nchan)$, with no predictive inner loop
(\Cref{app:exact-derivation}). Every step is then valid inference in the declared model: mean
field on the chain, where exact inference is intractable, and exact sum--product on the slot.

\subsection{Variants}
\label{sec:variants}

The clipped distance-sensitive arc score of Wu and Tu keeps only its $i-j>0$ half, with
$\clip+1$ buckets. All reported numbers use a layer-parallel schedule, which updates every
position from the previous iteration under a strictly lower-triangular mask: a depth-$\niter$
causal transformer with shared parameters (\Cref{app:serial}).

Two variants add variables. Wu and Tu propose global variables as an in-graph substitute for
the feed-forward block but report no results for them. A single-split global variable $\Gv{t}$, added as a leaf
off $\Zv{t}$, keeps the slot a tree and the model causal; its mean-field update is the
feed-forward-like operator $\sigma(q\Bglob^{\!\top}\!)\Bglob$ (\Cref{app:globals}).
\emph{Factored labels} replace the label variable by $\ncomp$ variables of width $\nlab/\ncomp$
that share $\Wv{t}$; the arc parameters fall with $\ncomp$ (\Cref{app:factored}). The slot is
still a tree, and the exact readout becomes a product of $\ncomp$ mixtures. Under the mean-field
readout the logits stay affine in the concatenated beliefs, so at a fixed total label width we
predict that factoring helps only through the exact readout.

Other extensions must also add variables: a multilayer perceptron on $\QZ{t}$ or untied
per-iteration parameters would add a map with no factor behind it.
