\section{Causal Probabilistic Transformers}
\label{sec:model}

We first recall the model of \citet{wu2023probabilistic} only as far as we need it
(\Cref{sec:recap}), then state the obstruction that prevents a direct causal version
(\Cref{sec:obstruction}), then give the model (\Cref{sec:chain}), its output mechanism
(\Cref{sec:output}), its inference procedure (\Cref{sec:inference}), and finally some variants.

\subsection{Background}
\label{sec:recap}

A probabilistic transformer is a conditional random field over two families of discrete latent
variables. Each position $i$ of a sentence carries a \emph{label} variable $\Zv{i}$ over $\nlab$
values, read as a syntactic category, and $\nchan$ \emph{head} variables $\Hv{i}{1..\nchan}$, one
per \emph{channel}, each over the candidate heads of $i$. A unary factor
$\exp(\Sfac_{w_i, \Zv{i}})$ ties the label at $i$ to the word observed there through a single
matrix $\Sfac \in \R^{|\Vocab| \times \nlab}$, and a ternary factor
$\exp\big(\ind{\Hv{i}{c}{=}j}\, \Tfac{c}_{\Zv{i},\Zv{j}}\big)$ scores the compatibility of the
labels at $i$ and at its head $j$ in channel $c$.

Running mean-field inference for $\niter$ iterations produces a computation graph in which the
head posteriors are softmaxes of bilinear forms in the label beliefs and the label beliefs are
softmaxes of the messages those posteriors weight: scaled dot-product attention followed by a
re-injected unary, with the same parameters at every iteration --- a $\niter$-layer transformer
encoder with shared weights and no feed-forward block. Self-attention is not a component that
was chosen; it is the posterior over syntactic heads.

The fact \Cref{sec:output} turns on is this: \textbf{the word $w_i$ enters the model in exactly
one place}, the unary factor $\Sfac_{w_i,\cdot}$. Nothing else in the energy mentions the words.

\subsection{Why a causal mask is not enough}
\label{sec:obstruction}

The obvious route to a causal model is to mask the ternary factors so that $\Hv{i}{c}$ may only
point to positions $j < i$. This does not give a causal model. Three properties one would want
cannot hold together:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
  \item \textbf{Single global energy (SE):} the updates are
        $\Q_x \propto \exp(-\nabla_x E / \lambda_x)$ for one energy $E$, with the words entering
        only through terms local to a single position;
  \item \textbf{Causality (C2):} for every schedule and every node, the update at position $i$
        does not read the state of any node at a position $j > i$;
  \item \textbf{Contextuality (X):} some belief at some position genuinely changes when a word
        at another position changes.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Trilemma]
\label{thm:trilemma}
No scheme satisfies SE, C2 and X simultaneously. Any two of the three are realisable.
\end{theorem}

The mechanism is Schwarz's theorem, not the partition function. If $E$ is twice continuously
differentiable then the mixed partial $\partial^2 E / \partial \Q_i \partial \Q_j$ is symmetric,
so \emph{every coupling in a single smooth energy is two-sided}: the term by which the label at
$j$ reaches the update at $i$ and the term by which the label at $i$ reaches the update at $j$
are two faces of one Hessian entry. Causality deletes one face and symmetry then deletes the
other, leaving an energy whose centred gradient at $i$ reads nothing outside position $i$ ---
which is the denial of contextuality. Masking the factors changes which configurations score
highly; it does not make the surviving coupling one-sided. The proof is in \Cref{app:proof}.

Two remarks, because both are places a reader will press. First, the theorem needs causality in
its \emph{architectural} form (C2 above); with the literal trajectory form --- ``$\Q_i^{(t)}$,
as a function of the words, does not depend on $w_j$ for $j>i$'' --- it is false, and
\Cref{app:proof} gives the counterexample: a polynomial energy on two binary positions whose
acausal coefficient is tuned to vanish at every state the dynamics can reach. C2 is what masked
attention satisfies for all parameters and all inputs, and it is what anyone means by a causal
architecture. Second, the theorem is about context \emph{mediated by inference}. An energy
allowed to contain word-nonlocal terms can be causal and contextual at once, by conditioning on
the prefix directly and bypassing inference altogether; excluding that is exactly the scope of
the word-locality assumption.

Causality and contextuality are what the model is for, so the single global energy is what we
give up.

\begin{remark}[Never formed, not deleted]
\label{rmk:never-formed}
Building the symmetric model and then deleting the anti-causal contribution by hand gives a
different object from building a model in which that contribution never exists: the first is not
the gradient field of any energy, and \Cref{thm:trilemma} says no repair of it can be. This
distinction is what \Cref{sec:chain} implements and what \Cref{sec:exp-correctness} tests.
\end{remark}

\subsection{A directed chain of conditional CRFs}
\label{sec:chain}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\input{figures/fig1_factor_graph}
\caption{The same factor graph in its two conditioning patterns, $\seqlen=3$. Shaded nodes are
observed. \textbf{(a)} Encoder: every word variable is clamped, arcs are undirected, and the
model is that of \citet{wu2023probabilistic}. \textbf{(b)} Decoder at slot $t=3$: the prefix
labels are frozen conditions (boxed), arcs reach backwards only, and the word variable $\Wv{3}$
is \emph{unclamped} --- its posterior is the next-token distribution. The factors and their
parameters are identical in the two panels; only what is conditioned on differs.}
\label{fig:factor-graph}
\end{figure}

Instead of one CRF over the sentence we define a chain of conditional CRFs, one per position.
The CRF at position $i$ ranges over $\big(\Zv{i}, \Hv{i}{1..\nchan}\big)$ and is conditioned on
the realised prefix labels $z_{\prefix{i}}$:
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:chain}
\begin{aligned}
  &p\big(\Zv{i}, \{\Hv{i}{c}\}_c \mid z_{\prefix{i}}, w_{1:i}\big) \\
  &\quad \propto
     \exp\Big(\Sfac_{w_i,\Zv{i}}
      + \textstyle\sum_{c}\sum_{j \in \Dep{i}} \ind{\Hv{i}{c}{=}j}\, \tau^{(c)}_{\Zv{i}}(j)\Big),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
with head domain $\Dep{i} = \{\textsc{root}, 1, \dots, i-1\}$, arc score
$\tau^{(c)}_a(j) = \Tfac{c}_{a, z_j}$ for $j<i$, and $\tau^{(c)}_a(\textsc{root}) = \rfac{c}_a$ a
learned root column. The joint over the sentence is the chain-rule product
$p(z, h \mid w) = \prod_i p(z_i, h_i \mid z_{\prefix{i}}, w_{1:i})$, a valid probability model by
construction. The root column makes $i=1$ well posed --- the first word attends to \textsc{root}
with probability one --- so the attention sink is part of the model rather than bolted on.

\paragraph{The anti-causal term is not born.}
In a directed model the factor in which position $i$ serves as the head of a later word $j$
lives inside $p(\Zv{j}, \Hvar{j} \mid z_{\prefix{j}})$, a conditional in which $z_i$ is a
conditioning value and not an inference variable. The local normaliser runs over
$(\Zv{j}, \Hvar{j})$ only, so no backward coupling arises from it either. \Cref{thm:trilemma} is
respected rather than contradicted: there is no single global energy here, and by the theorem
there cannot be.

\paragraph{Inference is variational filtering.}
Process $i = 1,\dots,\seqlen$, maintaining frozen filtering marginals
$\qbar{j} \approx p(\Zv{j} \mid w_{1:j})$ for $j<i$. Because the log-potential is linear in the
one-hot encoding of $z_j$, the expected log-conditional is exact at the potential level:
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:contract}
  \E_{\qbar{j}}\big[\Tfac{c}_{a,\Zv{j}}\big]
    = \textstyle\sum_b \qbar{j}(b)\, \Tfac{c}_{a,b} =: \Bfac{c}_{j,a}.
\end{equation}
%
Step $i$ is therefore exact mean-field inference on a small CRF with unary $\Sfac_{w_i,\cdot}$
and binary factors $\exp(\Bfac{c}_{j,a})$, with updates
%
\begin{align}
\label{eq:update-H}
  \QH{i}{c}(j) &\propto \exp\Big(\tfrac{1}{\lamH}\textstyle\sum_a \QZ{i}(a)\, \Bfac{c}_{j,a}\Big),\\
\label{eq:update-Z}
  \QZ{i}(a) &\propto \exp\Big(\tfrac{1}{\lamZ}\big[\Sfac_{w_i,a} + \mathcal{G}_i(a)\big]\Big),
  \nonumber\\
  \mathcal{G}_i(a) &= \textstyle\sum_c\sum_{j\in\Dep{i}} \QH{i}{c}(j)\, \Bfac{c}_{j,a}.
\end{align}
%
Once $\Tfac{c}$ is decomposed as $\Ufac{c}\Vfac{c}{}^{\top}$ this is masked scaled dot-product
attention verbatim: the query $\QZ{i}\Ufac{c}$ comes from the current token's evolving state,
and the keys $\qbar{j}\Vfac{c}$ come from frozen prefix states. \emph{Keys and values are the
same object.} The KV cache is a consequence of freezing the prefix rather than an engineering
trick, and three properties of the encoder's correspondence improve: the factor of two from
symmetrisation disappears; no symmetry assumption on $\Tfac{c}$ is needed, so this is a
genuinely directed dependency model rather than a link-grammar-style undirected one; and the
excluded diagonal is unproblematic because the re-injected unary plays the residual role.

\paragraph{Frozen forward, not frozen backward.}
\emph{Frozen} means constant with respect to step $i$'s inference problem, not detached in
autodiff. Training gradients flow backwards through $\Bfac{c}_{j,\cdot}$ into $\qbar{j}$ and
through the whole prefix computation, exactly as they do through cached activations in a causal
transformer. Forward causality is what defines a decoder; backward gradient flow is what trains
it, and it is how $\Sfac$ is trained in both of the roles \Cref{sec:output} gives it.

\subsection{The output mechanism}
\label{sec:output}

This is the part of the construction we most want checked, and we present it as such.

\paragraph{The design we rejected.}
The obvious way to obtain next-token logits is to project $\QZ{t}$ through a fresh
$\nlab \times |\Vocab|$ matrix and train with shifted targets. Three objections, all fatal. Such
a matrix corresponds to no factor in the graph, so the prediction would leave the model without
traversing it. $\QZ{t}$ is the posterior over the label of an \emph{already-known} word,
conditioned on $w_t$ through its own unary, so it answers ``what is the category of this known
word'', not ``what comes next''. And the arc factor connects the label of a word to the label of
its \emph{head}, never to the label of the next position, so no path in the graph leads from
$\Zv{t}$ to the identity of $w_{t+1}$. We report the rejected design because rejecting it is
what forced the mechanism below.

\paragraph{Unclamping the word variable.}
The repair is strongly constrained by the graph: the only factor touching a word variable is the
word--label factor, and the only factors touching a label are that one and its arcs, so any
graph-faithful route from prefix to vocabulary must be
$\qbar{\prefix{t}} \xrightarrow{\ \text{arcs}\ } \Zv{t} \xrightarrow{\ \Sfac\ } \Wv{t}$. Promote
each word to a variable and the chain becomes a joint over words,
$p(w,z,h) = \prod_t p(\Wv{t}, \Zv{t}, \Hvar{t} \mid z_{\prefix{t}})$, with step conditional
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:unclamp}
\begin{aligned}
 &p\big(\Wv{t}, \Zv{t}, \Hvar{t} \mid z_{\prefix{t}}\big) \propto
   \exp\Big(\underbrace{\bfac_{\Wv{t}}}_{\text{word unary}}
   + \underbrace{\Sfac_{\Wv{t},\Zv{t}}}_{\text{word--label factor}} \\[-4pt]
 &\qquad\qquad\quad
   + \textstyle\sum_c \sum_{j\in\Dep{t}} \ind{\Hv{t}{c}{=}j}\, \Tfac{c}_{\Zv{t}, z_j}\Big).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
%
No factor is added and no parameter is created. The unary $\exp(\Sfac_{w_t,\Zv{t}})$ of
\Cref{sec:recap} \emph{always was} the binary factor $\exp(\Sfac_{\Wv{t},\Zv{t}})$ with $\Wv{t}$
clamped to the observed word; making it latent unclamps a factor rather than adding one.
Next-token prediction is the posterior over a variable the model already had.

\paragraph{Consequences.}
Three properties follow, and they are consequences rather than design choices.
\textbf{(i) Tying is forced.} The step conditional contains exactly one word--label factor, and
the same $\Sfac$ mediates whether one conditions on $\Wv{t}{=}w_t$ or predicts it. Two matrices
for the two roles would be two factors on the same pair, and
$\exp(\Sfac_{w,a})\exp(\Sfac'_{w,a}) = \exp((\Sfac{+}\Sfac')_{w,a})$ collapses back to one.
Untying is not a hyperparameter; it is a different model.
\textbf{(ii) The output bias is the word unary.} $\bfac$ is a learned unigram potential on
$\Wv{t}$ and enters the logits as an additive per-word constant --- the bias of a language-model
head, as a factor. Setting $\bfac \equiv 0$ drops it.
\textbf{(iii) One model, two conditioning patterns.} The observed-mode energy is the
predictive-mode energy with $\QW{t}$ clamped to $\delta_{w_t}$; encoding and decoding are not two
models (\Cref{fig:factor-graph}).

\subsection{Inference at a slot, and the exact readout}
\label{sec:inference}

Fix slot $t$ with the prefix frozen and contracted into $\Bfac{c}_{j,a}$ by \Cref{eq:contract}.
The live variables are $\QW{t}$, $\QZ{t}$ and $\{\QH{t}{c}\}$; the updates of
\Cref{eq:update-H,eq:update-Z} gain a word update
$\QW{t}(w) \propto \exp\big(\tfrac{1}{\lamW}[\bfac_w + \sum_a \QZ{t}(a)\Sfac_{w,a}]\big)$, whose
softmax \emph{is} the language-model output layer. Initialisation follows the same rule as every
other variable --- each starts from its own unary-type factors --- giving
$\QW{t}^{(0)} \propto \exp(\bfac)$ and hence a fixed learned query
$\tilde{s}_a = \sum_w \QW{t}^{(0)}(w)\,\Sfac_{w,a}$, the prior-frequency-weighted mean embedding.
That is the \textsc{[mask]} token of masked language modelling, or the learned query embedding of
two-stream attention, derived rather than invented.

We do not use that readout as the mainline. The slot's factor graph is a star centred on
$\Zv{t}$ --- edges $\Wv{t}\!-\!\Zv{t}$ and $\Hv{t}{c}\!-\!\Zv{t}$ --- hence a tree, so
sum--product is exact and available in closed form.

\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:exact-readout}
For the slot conditional of \Cref{eq:unclamp} with the prefix frozen,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:exact-readout}
\begin{aligned}
  \hat{p}(\Wv{t}{=}w \mid w_{\prefix{t}}) &\propto e^{\bfac_w}\textstyle\sum_a e^{\Sfac_{w,a}}\readout{t}(a),\\
  \log \readout{t}(a) &= \textstyle\sum_{c=1}^{\nchan} \LSE_{j \in \Dep{t}} \Bfac{c}_{j,a},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with the log-sum-exp seeded at $j=\textsc{root}$ by $\rfac{c}_a$.
\end{proposition}

Two things about this form matter. It is a \emph{mixture of softmaxes} with $\nlab$ fixed
components and context-dependent weights, so it escapes the rank-$\nlab$ constraint that the
mean-field readout $\exp\E_{\QZ{t}}[\Sfac_{w,\cdot}]$ suffers by Jensen's inequality
(\Cref{sec:cmp-readout}). And along the sequence $\Dep{t}$ grows by one position per step, so
\Cref{eq:exact-readout} is a causal prefix log-sum-exp: one \texttt{logcumsumexp} scan,
$O(\seqlen\nlab\nchan)$, fully parallel over positions, leaving the layer-parallel training
schedule intact and removing the predictive inner loop entirely. Two costs are real and we state
them rather than bury them: the $|\Vocab|\times\nlab$ readout is a log-sum-exp rather than a
matmul --- the same FLOPs with worse hardware constants, chunked over the vocabulary like a fused
cross-entropy --- and the optimisation behaviour of log-sum-exp pooling gradients at
language-model scale is untested. \Cref{sec:exp3} is where we test it.

\paragraph{Every step is valid inference.}
Adopting \Cref{eq:exact-readout} changes what we claim in letter though not in substance: from
``every update is a softmax of a gradient of a per-step energy'' to \emph{every step is valid
inference in the declared model} --- mean field where exactness is impossible (the chain), exact
sum--product where the subgraph is a tree (the slot). No operation between corpus and loss fails
to be a message through a declared factor.

\subsection{Extensions and variants}
\label{sec:variants}

\paragraph{Distance.}
The clipped distance-sensitive arc score of \citet{wu2023probabilistic} carries over with only
its $i-j>0$ half, so there are $\clip+1$ buckets rather than $2\clip+1$ --- half the parameters.
\Cref{sec:exp-ablations} measures what the buckets buy.

\paragraph{Update schedule.}
Serial left-to-right with an inner loop per slot gives the clean filtering story; layer-parallel
--- iteration $t$ computes all $\QZ{i}^{(t)}$ from $\{\QZ{j}^{(t-1)}\}_{j<i}$ under one strict
lower-triangular mask --- has the computation graph of a depth-$\niter$, parameter-shared causal
transformer. Both are causal, and all reported numbers use the layer-parallel schedule.

\paragraph{Global variables.}
\citet{wu2023probabilistic} propose global variables in their Appendix B.3 as an in-graph
substitute for the missing feed-forward structure, and never test them. Adding a single-split
global variable $\Gv{t}$ as one more leaf off $\Zv{t}$ keeps the slot a tree, keeps causality,
and contributes a multiplicative term to $\readout{t}$; its mean-field update is exactly the
feed-forward-like operator $\sigma(q\Bglob^{\!\top}\!)\Bglob$. We treat it as a measured
variable (\Cref{sec:exp-ablations}), not an assumption.

\paragraph{Factored labels.}
The strongest graph-faithful widening replaces one label variable of width $\nlab$ by $K$ label
variables of width $\nlab/K$ sharing $\Wv{t}$, with each channel assigned a child and a head
component. The parameter count is unchanged, the slot is still a tree, and the exact readout
becomes a \emph{product} of $K$ mixtures --- a strictly richer family at the same width. Under
the mean-field readout it buys nothing, since the logits stay affine in the concatenated
beliefs; the gain, if any, exists only through \Cref{eq:exact-readout}. That is a sharp
prediction and \Cref{sec:exp-factored} tests it.

\paragraph{What is not allowed.}
Expressivity in this model class is bought by adding variables and factors, never by adding
maps. A multilayer perceptron from $\QZ{t}$ to the logits, a context-dependent $\Sfac$, a
nonlinearity applied to $\qbar{}$ between steps, or untied per-iteration parameters would each
reintroduce a neural head in probabilistic clothing. Further variants are in
\Cref{app:variants}.
