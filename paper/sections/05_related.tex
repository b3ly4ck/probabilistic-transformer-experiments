\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\citet{kuang2026scaling} transfer maximal update parameterisation \citep{yang2022tensor} to Wu
and Tu's encoder and find it ahead of a standard transformer at matched budget on masked language
modelling up to 0.4B parameters, which suggests that small-scale gaps partly reflect tuning; our
temperature correction is complementary to their rescaling. \citet{yang2018breaking} show that a linear--softmax head confines the log-conditionals to a
low-dimensional affine family and propose a mixture of softmaxes; our exact readout is such a
mixture, derived from inference on the slot tree and with no extra parameters. Tying input and
output embeddings is a known improvement for language models \citep{press2017using}; here it
follows from the factor graph. Syntactic language models condition generation on explicit trees
\citep{chelba1998exploiting,dyer2016recurrent,sartran2022transformer} or on latent tree
structure \citep{shen2019ordered}; our model keeps its syntax latent and never commits to a tree.
Probes find some dependency structure in trained transformers
\citep{hewitt2019structural,clark2019what,htut2019attention}, depending on the objective and the
probe \citep{ravishankar2021attention}, and other work induces trees jointly with a language
model \citep{shen2021structformer,shen2022udgn}. Our
head variable is an attachment by construction, and its clipped distance-sensitive arc scores
play the role of the relative position representations of \citet{shaw2018self}.
