\subsection{Which property to give up}
\label{sec:which-to-give-up}

Causality and contextuality are the point of the model, so the single global energy is what
goes. \Cref{sec:construction} replaces it with a directed chain of conditional CRFs.

\begin{remark}[Never formed, not deleted]
\label{rmk:never-formed}
The distinction matters and is easy to lose in implementation. Building the symmetric model and
then masking the anti-causal contribution gives a different object from building a model in
which that contribution never exists: the former still normalises over configurations that the
latter never admits. This is why the prefix must enter step $t$ as a \emph{frozen condition}
--- neither gradients nor messages flowing backwards --- and why we test for it directly
(\Cref{sec:exp0}).
\end{remark}
